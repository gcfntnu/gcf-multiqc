# Source attribution

The GCF-specific parsers originate in the GPL-licensed gcfntnu/MultiQC fork,
based on work by the MultiQC contributors and GCF contributors, including
Arnar Flatberg. The initial extraction uses fork revision
`d3fce9dab5b8522ad022f04161a145db121ad23d`.

This package adapts those reporting interfaces to upstream MultiQC 1.35 and
adds correctness fixes and synthetic regression tests. Upstream MultiQC itself
is installed as a dependency; its source files are not patched by this package.

Source: https://github.com/gcfntnu/MultiQC
Upstream: https://github.com/MultiQC/MultiQC
